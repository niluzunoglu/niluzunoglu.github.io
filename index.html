About Me

I'm a Software Design Engineer with 4.5+ years of experience building mission-critical systems. My work sits at the intersection of aerospace software, artificial intelligence, and reliable backend systems.

At Turkish Aerospace, I architect software for satellite ground segments — from telemetry processing to spacecraft command systems, all built under rigorous ECSS standards and CCSDS protocols. I've contributed to multiple satellite programs, including microsatellite initiatives.

Currently pursuing my M.Sc. in Computer Engineering at Yıldız Technical University, where my thesis focuses on deep learning for exoplanet detection using Kepler/TESS mission data. My research combines signal processing, neural networks, and scientific computing.

Beyond code, I'm fascinated by the cosmos — from analyzing light curves of distant stars to tracking F-16s at airshows. This curiosity drives everything I build.

Currently exploring: AI-driven space data analysis, transformer architectures for remote sensing, and the future of autonomous spacecraft systems.
